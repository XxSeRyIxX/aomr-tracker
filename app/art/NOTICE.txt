The badge and achievement images (art/badges.pack and art/achievements.pack, packed one PNG after another, with their
places in art/badges.json and art/achievements.json), the game's texts (data/game.json), Steam's achievement list for
the game (data/steam-schema.vdf) and the public lists of the game's servers (data/server.json: badges, their
challenges, achievement numbers and how many players have each) are NOT part of this project's source code and are
NOT covered by its GNU AGPL v3 licence. tools/export copies the first three from an installed copy of the game and
tools/server-data.mjs reads the last from the servers' public pages, so the website can show the badges, the
achievements, their names and goals without the game on hand. They must not be redistributed separately or treated as
freely licensed material.
